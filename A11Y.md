## Completed

- Main authenticated app shell now exposes a `main` landmark through `src/components/layout.rs`.
- Shared `Input` supports stable `id`, `name`, and `aria-label`; `SearchInput` now renders a screen-reader-only wrapped label.
- Axe-core Playwright coverage lives in `tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts`.
- The axe suite now has two modes:
  - with `SODMIN_E2E_ADMIN_EMAIL` / `SODMIN_E2E_ADMIN_PASSWORD`, it scans
    authenticated admin routes;
  - without credentials, it still scans the deployed public `/login` route
    instead of skipping the whole audit.
- Keyboard-only smoke coverage for devices, peers/federation, audit, and policy lives in `tests/e2e/keyboard-flows.spec.ts`.
- Form controls reviewed in the Phase 3 touched pages: trust-domain, relaxed-window, delivery-binding, realm-destroy, audit-attestation, realm-links, and server-status retain visible labels, accessible names, or structured landmarks.
